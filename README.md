# NEXT & Seek

The NEXTPredict Summit game: hide and seek inside Convene, 30 Hudson Yards, New York, the home of NEXTPredict Summit NYC on 22 to 23 October 2026.

**Play:** https://pierreobv.github.io/next-and-seek/

- `index.html` is the landing page: the game, how to play, playing together, our partners and the summit.
- `game.html` is the game itself, one self-contained file (three.js). It also runs offline when downloaded, except for online play.
- Online games go through the NEXT & Seek relay on Render. A link with `?room=ABCD` opens the page ready to join game ABCD.

Laptop or desktop with a mouse and keyboard, best in Chrome.
